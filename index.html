ABOUT.

The official page of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre of Viswajyothi College Of Engineering And Technology. We bridge the gap between ideas and execution.

Our primary objective is to cultivate an ecosystem that fosters innovation, empowers aspiring entrepreneurs, and transforms raw concepts into market-ready solutions. Through continuous learning, mentorship, and hands-on projects, IEDC VJCET stands as the bedrock of student-led technological advancement.

LIVE EVENTS.

System Ready. Engage. Execute.

Our live events are dynamic arenas where theoretical knowledge meets practical application. Ranging from high-stakes hackathons to intensive pitch deck competitions and AI-driven workshops, these sessions are designed to push boundaries.
