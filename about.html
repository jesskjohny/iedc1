MISSION.

The official page of Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre of Viswajyothi College Of Engineering And Technology. We bridge the gap between ideas and execution.

The Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Centre (IEDC) at Viswajyothi College of Engineering and Technology (VJCET), established in 2017, inspires both students and faculty to explore innovation and entrepreneurship. Join hands with IEDC to bring your creative ideas to life, sharpen your technical skills, and pursue startup dreams through engaging programs, interactive workshops, and meaningful industry collaborations.

LIVE EVENTS.

System Ready. Engage. Execute.

Our live events are dynamic arenas where theoretical knowledge meets practical application. Ranging from high-stakes hackathons like HCK-09 to intensive pitch deck competitions (PCH-XR) and AI-driven workshops (WKS-AI), these sessions are designed to push boundaries. We provide the platform; you provide the ingenuity.
